Trust this push gateway?

Your homeserver will send {{ host() }} a notification every time you receive a message, on every account signed in to this device. Its operator will be able to see:

  • your Matrix ID
  • that every account on this device belongs to one person
  • which room each message arrived in, and exactly when
  • your device's notification token

They will not be able to read your messages — Trinity sends only a reference to each message, never its content — and they cannot send notifications to your device without your app's push credentials. Only use a gateway you run yourself or otherwise trust.
